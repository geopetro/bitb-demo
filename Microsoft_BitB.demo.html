Strategic Project Proposal — Confidential

Prepared for the Executive Committee · Q4 Review · Internal use only

This document outlines the proposed scope, budget, and delivery timeline for the next phase of the transformation programme. The contents are commercially sensitive and must not be distributed outside the approved circulation list.

Following the review conducted over the previous quarter, the steering group has identified three priority workstreams. Each is summarised below together with the associated resourcing requirements and expected return on investment.

1. Programme Objectives

The primary objective is to consolidate the regional platforms onto a single operating model, reducing duplicated effort and improving reporting accuracy. Secondary objectives include strengthening controls and shortening the monthly close cycle by a target of four working days.

2. Budget Summary

The indicative budget assumes a phased rollout across four quarters, with the majority of capital expenditure falling in the first half. A detailed breakdown by cost centre is provided in Appendix A.

Approval is requested to proceed to the detailed design stage, subject to the conditions set out in the governance section.
